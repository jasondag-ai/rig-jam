# Rig Jam: Art Bible

Renamed from Rush Hour Rigs on Oct 9, 2026 (trademark).

Every image for the game follows this file. If an image breaks a rule here, it is rejected. Gag rules, screen zones and camera rules live in GAME_BIBLE.md.

## 1. Style

- Stylized Pixar-like 3D. Match the cover image (the roughneck with the pipe wrench): exaggerated proportions, expressive comedic faces, realistic materials (FR cotton, hi-vis fabric, weathered steel, rubber, fur), soft warm cinematic lighting from the top left.
- Light weathering: dust, a little mud, worn paint edges. Never heavy rust or junk.
- Colors bright and saturated, but believable. Equipment colors follow the specs below.
- **The board (Jay, Oct 4): table-top toy look.** Everything on the board and the lease surroundings (pad, berm, gates, trees, bushes, equipment, tracks) is a flat-shaded, chunky toy with a dark outline matching the trucks, one light from the top left, low-frequency colour fields, no photographic texture or grain. The trucks are the reference. The cover image stays Pixar 3D (it is a poster). Equipment keeps its 3/4 camera but is drawn in the same flat outlined language. Season overlays (snow, mud) are clipped to the sprite shape.

## 2. Technical rules (every file)

- Transparent PNG background, except ground and grass textures.
- No ground shadow, no glow, no halo. The game adds shadows.
- No text, numbers, logos, brand names or real grille designs anywhere. Signs are blank.
- Camera:
  - **Top-down** (straight down, no perspective): trucks, ground, grass, the lease berm, gates.
  - **3/4 high angle**: everything that stands up (equipment, scenery, characters, props).
- One version per item. Use the exact file names given.
- Animation clips follow GAME_BIBLE.md section 4 (in-place rule, continuity rule, camera per zone).

## 3. Accuracy spec: Alberta oilfield

### The lease
- **Lease perimeter:** a dirt berm (a low raised mound of pushed-up topsoil and subsoil) around the pad, with a rounded top and sloped sides. Grass and weeds on the outer slope. No pipe-rail fence, no wood, no chain link: drilling leases are bermed, not fenced.
  - Summer: brown dirt berm with grass tufts. Spring: wet dark mud berm. Winter: snow-covered berm.
- **Gates:** pipe swing gates set into gaps in the berm at the entrances and exits, welded hinges, chain latch. The game tints each gate to its truck color and adds the color-blind symbol.
- **Pad surface:** packed gravel (summer), mud (spring), packed snow (winter). One continuous surface, never visibly tiled.

### Equipment
- **Wellhead (production tree):** casing head flange at ground level, stacked master valve(s) with round handwheels, a tee or cross with one wing valve leading to a short flowline, a pressure gauge on top. Usually red or grey. Sits on gravel, often with yellow guard posts. **It is NOT a fire hydrant.**
- **Pumpjack (beam pumping unit):** skid base, A-frame samson post, walking beam, horsehead on the well end with a wireline bridle, carrier bar and polished rod going down into a stuffing box on the wellhead, pitman arms, crank arms with counterweights, gearbox, electric motor with belt guard. Common colors: grey beam, orange or red horsehead. Motion spec: GAME_BIBLE.md section 7.
- **400 bbl tank:** vertical welded steel tank, about as tall as it is wide, shallow cone roof, thief hatch and vent on the roof, ladder or stairs with a small railed walkway, load line valve near the bottom. Tan, grey or green paint. Welded seams, no rivets.
- **Flare stack:** tall thin steel stack on a concrete pad, ladder, guy wires, knockout drum at the base, small pilot flame at the tip.

### Vehicles (top-down, cab facing up, cab body painted plain WHITE so the game can tint it)
- **Pickup:** 3/4 or 1-ton crew cab, open box, toolbox across the front of the box, light bar, buggy whip flag mount, mud flaps.
- **Picker truck:** straight truck with flat deck and a folded knuckle-boom crane behind the cab, outriggers stowed.
- **Vac truck:** round tank with rear dome hatch, hose trays down the sides, vacuum pump behind the cab.
- **Frac pump truck:** tractor or straight truck carrying a large triplex or quintuplex pump and diesel engine with radiator, discharge iron on the side.
- **Water hauler:** smooth oval tank, top walkway with hatches, side ladder, rear valves and hose.
- **Hotshot:** 1-ton dually pickup towing a gooseneck flatdeck trailer.

### People (PPE is never optional)
- **All field workers:** FR coveralls (red or navy) with reflective stripes, hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, steel-toe boots, a small gas monitor clipped at the chest.
- **Spotter:** same, plus a hi-vis vest and two orange flags.
- **Company man:** white hard hat, FR shirt and jeans or coveralls, clipboard, grumpy.
- **Landowner:** older rancher, cowboy hat, plaid shirt, jeans, work boots, rides a quad. Not a cartoon oil baron.

### Wildlife and scenery
- **Black bear, moose, magpie, Richardson's ground squirrel, Canada goose, porcupine:** true to the real Alberta animal, stylized.
- **Trees:** white spruce, trembling aspen (white bark), willow shrubs. Prairie grass and slough cattails are allowed.
- **Biffy:** blue portable toilet with white roof.

## 4. Never draw

Pipe-rail lease fences, fire hydrants, traffic cones, construction barrels, concrete culvert pipes, excavators, tower cranes, hard-hat-less workers, oil gushers, wooden derricks, cartoon oil barons, red oil drums as main props, palm trees, desert, cacti, any text or logo.

## 5. Approved art in the game (Oct 3)

World: spruce, aspen, willow, cattails, blank lease sign. UI: buttons, icons, badge, streak sign, win panel, level cards. Trucks: pickup, picker, vac, frac, water hauler. Equipment: pumpjack, 400 bbl tank, wellhead (3/4 view). Cover: front power stance roughneck. Model sheets: spotter, bear, rabbit, magpie, moose.
