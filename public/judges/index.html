How to play

  1. Drag a truck along its length. It slides until something stops it.
  2. Drive it out the gate of its own colour. Any other gate is a wall.
  3. One drag is one move. Clear the pad in par moves for three hard hats.
  4. Undo and Restart are free. Hint shows the best next move. Flick a truck to send it all the way.

What to look for

Seven regions, one new rule each

  • 1Cardium: trucks and gates
  • 2Montney: lease equipment in the way
  • 3Duvernay: convoys leave in number order
  • 4Mannville: muskeg, you slide until you hit something
  • 5Bakken: load racks and shift-change gates
  • 6Clearwater: the 8 x 8 Big Pad
  • 7Baldonnel: road bans, rigs can't cross soft ground
  • 40 sightings. Tap the scenery, bump the berm, try things. Each one goes in the Wildlife Log (the binoculars), which digs down through real Alberta geology and out the far side of the Earth.
  • A Daily Pad every day and a Sunday Turnaround every week, with a Days Without Incident streak and a share card.
  • Sound starts off. The gear turns it on. Four music styles.
  • Every par is the true minimum, proven by a solver.

Made for phones, held upright. On a laptop it plays in a phone-sized column with the mouse.
